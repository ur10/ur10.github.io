Overview

A frozen, simulation-pretrained 3D Diffusion Policy can bring a ring to the peg and still miss the final alignment under depth noise, occlusion and calibration error.

I developed a bounded SAC residual controller that corrects the base policy's 6-DoF actions using real-robot experience. A learned Q-advantage gate decides when a correction should be applied, and two RealSense cameras provide the fused point-cloud observation. The project includes physical UR10 runs and a complete perception-to-policy execution pipeline.

Method

Why a frozen 3D base

A point cloud directly represents observed surfaces in 3D, which exposes the relative pose, clearance and approach direction that alignment depends on. The DP3-style base keeps that geometric prior; the residual builds on it rather than retraining it.

Why a gate

A residual that always acts can undo good base behaviour. Twin Q-functions score the corrected action against the base action, and the gate opens only when the correction is predicted to help, with hysteresis to prevent rapid switching.
