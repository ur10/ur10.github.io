About

I'm an MS Robotics researcher at Northeastern University working on robot learning, sim-to-real transfer and optimization-based methods for reliable autonomy. I build robot-learning systems, use simulation and optimization to address their limitations, and test whether those improvements transfer to physical hardware. Currently I work on:

  • Force-aware residual RL for contact-rich insertion on a physical UR5e
  • Real-to-sim data generation: robot demonstrations synthesized from reconstructed 3D scenes, with collision-aware trajectory repair
  • Certifiable state estimation: semidefinite relaxations that certify a globally optimal IMU–GNSS estimate

Before graduate school I deployed real-time MPC on production UR5 arms at Mowito, and in 2025 I worked on policy distillation and hardware evaluation for a 7-DoF arm at ManiroAI. I'm happy to talk about robot learning, manipulation and state estimation.

Optimization & Estimation

A QCQP-Representable IMU Pre-Integration Factor for Certifiable State Estimation

Utkarsh Rai, Zhexin Xu, Bang-Shien Chen, David Rosen

arXiv preprint, 2026  EuRoC + UrbanNav IMU

34/34 EuRoC flight windows and 122/143 UrbanNav windows certified globally optimal

A Cayley-based preintegration factor turns IMU–GNSS smoothing into a QCQP. When its semidefinite relaxation is tight, the estimate comes with a certificate of global optimality, where local solvers can stall 158° off.
